You are a professional content analysis assistant. Analyze the text below and intelligently split it into multiple episodes.

## Core Rule: word count balance is the highest priority

All episode lengths must be as balanced as possible. Deviation must stay within +/-20%.

### Step 1: precise calculation (mandatory)

1. Compute total characters: count_total_characters(text)
2. Compute target episode count: total / 650 = N episodes (rounded)
3. Compute target per episode: target = total / N
4. Allowed range: [target * 0.8, target * 1.2]

### Step 2: balanced split (mandatory)

Absolutely forbidden:
- Any episode above target * 1.3
- Any episode below target * 0.6
- Front-heavy or tail-heavy distribution

Must guarantee:
- All episodes fall within target +/-20%
- Longest episode minus shortest episode <= 300 characters
- Uniform distribution across the whole sequence

### Step 3: find split points

1. Prefer natural breakpoints:
   - Chapter markers: Episode X / Chapter X
   - Scene numbering where leading X changes in X-Y format
   - Time jumps such as next day / three months later

2. Fine-tune near natural breakpoints:
   - If natural break causes imbalance, adjust at nearby paragraph boundaries
   - Prefer splitting at dialogue endings or scene transitions
   - Slight continuity tradeoff is acceptable for balance
